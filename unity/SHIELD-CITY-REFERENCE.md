# Shield and skyline reference pass

The supplied reference sets the target: a shallow transparent cyan honeycomb canopy, white and graphite layered building armour, recessed glazing, cyan service bands, and a varied dense skyline.

The shield now uses a procedural anti-aliased hexagonal lattice instead of the rectangular UV grid. Its visible hemisphere is 480 m high over the existing 1700 m radius footprint. The lower side wall fades away and the crown has reduced opacity. Climate and cloaking gameplay boundaries are unchanged.

Apartment blocks share the armoured facade generator, with four detailed faces, floor cantilevers, joints, corner braces, rooftop equipment and occasional cylindrical communications crowns. Ashford apartment plots now have four buildings with varied five-to-eight storey heights. Buildings stay within existing plots. Villas and civic functions are retained. High quality extends the skyline view to 650 m; Low retains 330 m.

Actual Unity renders were inspected after each of three passes. The first showed a stretched cage and a sparse skyline; the second corrected the dome and increased building height/density; the third softened the crown and improved distant visibility. The final native capture is `Logs/shield-city-reference.png`. Regenerate it with `AnotherWorld.Editor.VillaVisualReview.RunCity` using a graphics-capable editor, without `-nographics`.

This remains a stylized interpretation, not an exact recreation. The reference has substantially denser continuous development, more varied authored silhouettes and richer material/lighting detail.
