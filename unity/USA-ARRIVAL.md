USA arrival district and government transit
==========================================

Public Unity preview: https://hidrok-unity.vercel.app. The browser checks passed factory production and collection, USA travel and return, and reported no browser errors. Hosting verification confirmed the published assets match that tested build. This preview uses its own Vercel project.

Use the map to travel to **Government teleport circle** inside **Ashford Capital Government Offices**. Stand within the illuminated ring, press E, and choose the USA destination. The return circle in Washington, D.C. brings you back to the government hall. Vehicles, transit passengers and wanted players cannot teleport. Destination capsules are checked before transfer; teleporting never clears a police pursuit.

The USA region has its own visible scenery, map destinations and temperate climate. Antarctic exposure applies outside Hidrok, but not in the USA arrival district. Factories continue their active jobs while the player is abroad.

This is a compact National Mall arrival district, not a completed nationwide USA map. It preserves the west-to-east sequence of Lincoln Memorial, reflecting pool, Washington Monument and United States Capitol, with an abbreviated north/south arrangement of avenues and nearby buildings. Landmarks and distances are simplified for a walkable browser level. No official map imagery, textures or models are bundled.

Geographic references:
- National Park Service, National Mall and Memorial Parks: https://www.nps.gov/nama/index.htm
- NPS maps: https://www.nps.gov/nama/planyourvisit/maps.htm
- Washington Monument maps: https://www.nps.gov/wamo/planyourvisit/maps.htm

The teleport technology and its public arrival plaza are fictional additions to this setting.
