# Character credit

The game uses the Soldier and Michelle characters, and Soldier's Idle, Walk and Run animations from Mixamo, supplied in the Three.js r180 examples. Imported meshes are cloned with independent skeletons for each resident and the player. Clothing tints, animation transfer between the rigs, and first-person head masking are modifications for this prototype.

- Model source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
- Three.js example identifying Mixamo as the model source: https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_animation_skinning_blending.html
- Michelle source and Mixamo attribution: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/webgpu_skinning.html
- Adobe permits royalty-free incorporation of Mixamo characters and animations in video games: https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

The characters are not Poly Haven assets and are not asserted to be CC0 or covered by Three.js's software MIT license. They are incorporated into this game rather than offered as an asset pack.
