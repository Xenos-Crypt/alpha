# Hidrok visual direction

The user clarified that Another World is an advanced civilization. Preserve the
Unity world's identity while increasing material, lighting and geometric realism.

Reviewed local sources:

- `../unity/References/Landscaping-concept.png` and `Transport-concept.png` are
  design targets, not screenshots of the implemented game.
- `../unity/Logs/reference-city.png` and
  `web-desktop-villa-exterior-high.png` show the implemented Unity version.
- `FutureArchitecture.cs`, `HidrokClimate.cs`, `IntercityMaglev.cs`,
  `GLASS-VILLAS.md` and `SHIELD-CITY-REFERENCE.md` describe the architecture and systems.

Key features are layered pearl/white ceramic and graphite construction, recessed
panoramic glazing, precise cyan service lighting, green public spaces, glass
pavilions and reflecting pools. Transport uses levitation and elevated maglev
infrastructure. A shallow transparent honeycomb climate/cloaking canopy covers
Hidrok; its Unity footprint radius is 1700 m and height is 480 m.

Photorealism should come from credible scale, authored silhouettes, material
detail, lighting and movement. Do not substitute an old European brick streetscape
or equate a cyan overlay with a complete futuristic city.

`HidrokFuture` is the initial procedural translation. All 177 plot locations are
retained, with additional detail on the 12 nearest plots and two glass pavilions.
Orion now has an underground regional route and an elevated tram route with boarding, braking and station access (ORION-TRANSIT.md). The canopy remains a visual prototype. Everyday cars include the Aurora X9, Skyline X7 and Aerodynex X12
references (References/Aurora-X9.png, Variant-A.png and Variant-B.png). Their
different body shells, cabin layouts, folding fan layouts and gear are playable. Road hover, flight, basic energy use, landing and player
entry/exit are implemented. Autonomous navigation, full safety systems, passenger
interaction, climate/cloaking logic and pavilion traversal remain pending.
Planting is an early grass/planter pass; authored trees and flowers are pending.

Inspect actual in-game captures against the local references after visual changes.
Report gaps honestly. DX11 compatibility captures on Intel HD 530 do not establish
Bodycam-equivalent fidelity or smooth performance on other hardware.
