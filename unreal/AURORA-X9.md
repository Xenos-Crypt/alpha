# Aurora X9 everyday car

Reference: https://chatgpt.com/s/m_6ada4b2b0a6c8191b1bbd8adcfe8c8b9
Local board: References/Aurora-X9.png. Original project meshes are generated by Tools/make-transport-models.mjs and imported by Tools/import-transport-models.ps1.

The playable prototype has a 5.8 m body, approximately 6.2 m deployed width, four-seat visible cabin, two front lift fans and four outboard motors, folding wings, retractable landing gear, carbon/metal/glass materials and cyan lighting. Road/flight speed caps are 250/320 km/h with boost; climb input is 5 m/s. Energy storage is 180 kWh with a simplified 120 kW hydrogen fuel-cell contribution. These are gameplay approximations, not engineering simulation.

E enters/exits nearby cars; WASD drives; Shift boosts; F toggles flight/request landing; Space/Q ascends/descends; L toggles gear; C switches camera. Stop, land and fold before leaving.

Not yet implemented: full autonomous flight/terrain avoidance, voice/gesture or remote control, passenger occupancy, functional shielding/parachute/fire suppression, diagnostics, detailed powertrain thermodynamics and certified flight physics. The mesh is an authored procedural interpretation and still needs further surface/detail work to match the board's render fidelity.
